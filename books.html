By Your Cider

Out Sept 1st 2026

She's stuck working and (accidentally) living with a charming cider maker.

Harlow Spencer has a plan. Move to Ottawa, Canada, work in a dreamy small town nearby, and settle into a comfortable life with her boyfriend. It's perfect. Until she finds him cheating.

So Harlow sets off for Autumn Creek, despite knowing she'll never belong in a small town like this. But her two-month contract is the only stability she has left. Without a place to stay, Harlow lands in a spare room on an apple farm. Only now, she's living under the roof of an infuriatingly attractive man who gets along with everyone but her.

Jude Bramley isn't expecting the woman who moved into his house without saying hello to be his new coworker. He certainly doesn't trust her to plan the Harvest Festival with him either. But to save his struggling town, they'll need to work together.

As fall unfolds, Jude shows Harlow the town he loves through his eyes. Surrounded by apple orchards and autumn leaves, their bickering turns into sizzling tension. But with the festival to focus on and their time together burning out, they must fight their attraction.

Or at least, they should.
